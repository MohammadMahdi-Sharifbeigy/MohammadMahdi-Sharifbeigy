## Featured research

### 1. [Latent Mean–Covariance Model of Prefrontal Population Activity](https://github.com/MohammadMahdi-Sharifbeigy/cov-mean-model)
`Population coding` `Gaussian processes` `Low-rank covariance` `SHAP` `Macaque dlPFC`

Transformer **mean** model + low-rank latent **covariance** with Gaussian-process temporal kernels, predicting a Gaussian over population spike counts.
- Interpreted with **null-standardized permutation SHAP**
- Validated on **7 steps of synthetic data**, including a known **Kronecker covariance** that the model recovered

### 2. [KG-GT v2: EEG-to-EMG Decoding](https://github.com/MohammadMahdi-Sharifbeigy/eeg-emg-grasp-decoder/tree/KG-GT-v2)
`EEG` `EMG` `Graph-Transformer` `Null tests` `WAY-EEG-GAL, 12 subjects`

Three separate outputs (**kinematics-only**, **EEG-only**, **fusion**) to ask one question: *what does EEG add beyond kinematics?*
- Kinematics output beat the baseline in **12 / 12** subjects (VAF **75.8%**)
- EEG-only output passed a time-shift null test in **12 / 12** subjects
- Fusion added almost nothing, and I report it as such &nbsp;·&nbsp; *work in progress*

### 3. [Few-Shot Ordinal CLIP for Diabetic Retinopathy](https://github.com/MohammadMahdi-Sharifbeigy/fewshot-ordinal-clip-dr)
`CLIP` `LoRA` `Ordinal losses` `Few-shot` `DeepDRiD`

QWK **0.759** with 32 images per grade (baseline 0.664) and **0.870** with full data.
